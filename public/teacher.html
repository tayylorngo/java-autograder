Students type their name, form teams of 2 in the lobby and press Ready. Start locks teams and opens uploads (the button shows how many teams are ready). Stop pauses uploads; Start again resumes the same timer. Back to lobby keeps the teams but clears scores and the timer. Reset everything also empties the lobby, so students have to type their names again.  Projector view · Download CSV

Manage teams

Move a student to another team or a new solo team, or remove them (they go back to the name screen).

Type the password to see the lobby.
