Students type their name, pick one of the fixed teams (2 per team) and press Ready. Start locks teams and opens uploads (the button shows how many teams are ready). Stop pauses uploads; Start again resumes the same timer. Back to lobby keeps the teams but clears scores and the timer. Reset everything also empties the lobby, so students have to type their names again.  Projector view · Download CSV

Manage teams

Move a student to another team (or off their team), or remove them (they go back to the name screen).

Type the password to see the lobby.
